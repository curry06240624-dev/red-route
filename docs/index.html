How to use Red Route

Plan a walk on Mars and check, at every step, that the crew can still get home.

  1. 1
    Check the planThree green PASS checks mean the crew can always get home. Leave each stop before its "Leave by" time.
  2. 2
    Walk the planPress Walk the plan. The blue dot is the crew; the green area is where they can still get home from. Astronaut view shows the crew's own screen.
  3. 3
    Change itDrag a numbered stop on the map, or open Plan your EVA. Every check updates at once.
